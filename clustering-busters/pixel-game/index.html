Ringfall

Mossback stole the village treasury. Fly in by catapult and get every ring back.

or press F  ·  M mutes

Controls

  • WASD walk
  • Mouse aims, Click or Space attacks
  • Q switches weapon, 1–8 cast specials
  • E talk, shop, launch

How to play

  • Train with Sergeant Brann, then buy gear from the merchants for rings.
  • The catapult throws you into Mossback's lair: beat his three stages, take the key from his neck.
  • Lose and he bounces you home. Keep the rings you found, grow the village, try again.
