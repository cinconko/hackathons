The treasury under the hill

Every ring Ringfall ever found sleeps in one treasury. Classic, rare and super rare: the village spends them, the village grows.

Then Mossback came

One night the hill shook. Mossback carried the chests off to his mountain and locked them in a room. The only key hangs around his neck.

The old catapult

No road climbs his mountain. But Grandpa's old catapult throws you straight into the lair. Sergeant Brann trains you first.

Three stages of Mossback

Every attack is warned by a red line or circle first. Read it, step out of it.

Mossback

A ring of balls in every direction. Stand in the gaps.

Angry

Aimed shotgun throws from his fist. Faster, harder.

Furious

Spirals, charges, a giant ball and a slam that rains balls.

Six merchants, six robes

After every fight a new merchant sets out his rug. His robe tells you what he sells.

How lucky are you?

Every chance in Ringfall, straight from the game's own rules. The sprites are the game's own too.

Controls

  • WASD walk
  • Mouse aims, Click / Space attacks
  • Q weapon, 1-8 specials, E use
  • F fullscreen, M mute

How to play

  • Train with Sergeant Brann, buy gear for rings.
  • The catapult throws you into the lair: three stages, then the key.
  • Lose and he bounces you home. Keep your rings, grow, try again.
